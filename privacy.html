Envelope

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Envelope is built so that we know as little about you as possible. This page says what the app and our servers handle, who else sees something, and what you can ask us to delete.

In short. Your encryption keys stay on your phone. Amounts you send are encrypted on Solana, so nobody, including us, can read them. We don’t sell data, show ads or build profiles.

What stays on your phone

  • The keys that lock and unlock your private balance. They are derived from a signature by your wallet and are never sent to us. Where your phone supports it, the signature is kept in secure storage behind your fingerprint or face unlock, so you don’t sign again each time.
  • A local, encrypted copy of your decrypted activity, so your history loads quickly.
  • Your list of pots and gift records.

Removing the app deletes all of it. Your funds are not affected, because they live on the blockchain.

What our servers receive

The app talks to a service we run, the relayer, which is hosted by Fly.io. To do its job it receives:

  • Your wallet address and the transactions you ask it to co-sign or submit, such as a private send, a refill, a plan purchase, a gift link or a statement. Amounts you send privately are encrypted and not readable by us.
  • Your IP address, which is used briefly to limit abuse and appears in our host’s standard connection logs.
  • Your push notification token and wallet address, if you allow notifications. We keep this in memory to tell your phone “you received a payment”. The notification never contains an amount. It is cleared when the service restarts, and the app registers it again.
  • Free-refill records: which wallets have had a refill, when, and the gas-tank address tied to each wallet. On mainnet we may also record that a Seeker Genesis Token has been used for a free refill.
  • Counts for our dashboard: numbers such as sends, refills and gift links. A wallet counted there is stored only as a salted, shortened hash, which can count wallets but can’t list or identify them. No address, recipient or amount is stored in these counts.
  • Statements: when you ask us to seal one, we receive only a fingerprint (a hash) of it, never the figures. The statement itself lives in the link you share.
  • The contact form: the name, email and message you type, kept until we delete it.

What is public on the blockchain

Solana is a public ledger and its records can’t be deleted. It shows which addresses made a transfer and when, fees and plan payments, and that pots and gift links exist. It does not show amounts of private transfers, which are encrypted. If you share a statement, whoever has the link sees the figures you included.

Who else is involved

  • Solana RPC provider. Your app reads the blockchain through our relayer, which forwards the requests to a node provider (Helius, or an equivalent). The provider sees the addresses looked up, but the requests come from our server, so your IP address isn’t passed on to it. The statement verify page, which runs in your browser, reads from the public network directly.
  • Expo’s push notification service delivers notifications to your phone.
  • Your wallet app (for example Solflare) handles your signatures under its own policy.
  • Fly.io hosts the relayer. GitHub hosts this website and the app downloads.
  • A public price feed (Jupiter) is read by our server to price refills. It receives no user data.

We don’t share data with advertisers, and the app has no advertising or third-party analytics kits.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: only to scan a QR code. Images are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Biometrics: only to unlock on your phone. We never receive your fingerprint or face data.
  • Notifications: only to tell you about incoming payments.

How long we keep things

  • Counts and refill records are kept while the service runs, so limits and the dashboard keep working.
  • Contact messages are kept until we delete them or you ask us to.
  • Connection logs are kept by our host for a short time under its own settings.

Your choices

  • Turn off notifications in your phone’s settings, or remove the app, to stop us holding your push token.
  • Delete my data, in the app’s Settings: your wallet signs a request and we remove your push notification token and unlink your usage records from your wallet. We keep a refill history for your wallet, because it is what stops the free first network-fee refill being claimed twice. It holds your wallet address and when refills were made, nothing more.
  • Ask us to delete a contact message, or anything else tied to your wallet that we can delete, through the contact form. We can’t change what is on the blockchain.
  • Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Use the same contact form to ask.

Children

Envelope is not for anyone under 18, and we don’t knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect, we’ll say so in the app or on our website.

Contact

Questions about your data? Write to us through the contact form on our website. Our Terms of Use apply to your use of Envelope.
